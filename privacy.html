Privacy Policy

SentenceRay · Last updated 24 August 2026

SentenceRay ("the app") is made by StarNova. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what your choices are. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us a name, an email address or a password to use the app.
  • The sentences you submit are sent to our servers and to our AI provider so they can be analysed, and the analysis is cached so your history works.
  • We keep an anonymous identifier for your install so your daily free allowance and your Pro subscription follow you.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

DataWhy
The text you submit — the sentence you paste, share into the app, or ask a question aboutTo produce the X-Ray (structure, backbone, meaning, vocabulary, quick check) and to answer your "Ask about this" questions. The text and its analysis are stored so your Recent and Saved lists work.
An anonymous visitor identifier — a random ID created on first launch, plus a device fingerprint value used only to recognise a reinstall as the same visitorTo count your free daily X-Rays and AI questions, to keep your history, and to attach a Pro subscription to your install. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number.
Preferences — your English level and your explanation languageTo pitch explanations at the right level and in the language you chose.
Usage events — for example: the app was opened, a sentence was X-Rayed, the paywall was shown, a purchase completedTo understand which parts of the app help and which do not. The text of your sentences is never included in analytics events.
Purchase state — whether a subscription is active, its product and renewal dateTo unlock Pro and to restore it on a new device.

SentenceRay does not ask for and does not use your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, location, calendar, or health data. It does not track you across other apps or websites, and it shows no advertising.

Who processes it

  • StarNova's own backend (the SentenceRay service) — stores your submissions, their analyses and your usage counters.
  • Our AI provider — receives the sentence text (and your question, if you ask one) in order to generate the analysis or answer. It is sent for processing only, without your identity attached.
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase — receives the usage events above, keyed to the anonymous visitor identifier.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription state. It receives an anonymous purchase identifier derived from your visitor ID.
  • Apple — handles the payment itself. We never see your payment details.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Submitted sentences and their analyses are kept while your install exists so that your history works; on the Free plan only your most recent five are shown. Analytics events are retained by Google for up to 14 months. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete the visitor record, its submissions and its analyses within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your data — email vdnhan97@gmail.com from any address and describe your install; we will delete it. Deleting the app also stops any further collection.
  • Manage your subscription — Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Brazil, or California) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing. Write to the address below and we will honour it.

Children

SentenceRay is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like their data removed, contact us and we will remove it.

International transfers

Our servers and our providers are located outside your country in some cases. Where data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, it is done under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, note it in the app.
