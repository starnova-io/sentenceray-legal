SentenceRay

X-Ray any English sentence: structure, backbone, meaning.
Understand the sentence, not just the words.

What it does

Paste a sentence you don't understand — or share it from any app — and SentenceRay breaks it into coloured chunks, shows the backbone underneath, explains why each part is there, pulls out words worth remembering, and checks that it clicked. Explanations are available in 11 languages.
